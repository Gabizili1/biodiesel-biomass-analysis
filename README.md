# Comparative Study of Biodiesels Produced from Waste of Beef Tallow, Avocado Pear, Watermelon and Desert Date

## 📄 Publication / Citation
This research has been published in the *International Journal of Modern Science and Technology (IJMST)*.
* **Citation:** Agbaye, G., Ogo, O., & Bawa, I. (2019). Comparative Study of Biodiesels Produced from Waste of Beef Tallow, Avocado Pear, Watermelon and Desert Date as Alternative Source of Energy. *IJMST*, 4(6), 138–147.
* 👉 **[Read the Full Published Paper Here](https://ijmstpublications.com)**

## 🔬 Project Overview
This project investigates the optimization and chemical synthesis of Fatty Acid Methyl Esters (FAME) via NaOH-catalyzed transesterification from alternative biomass wastes:
* Waste beef tallow fat
* Avocado seed pulp
* Watermelon seeds (*Citrullus lanatus*)
* Desert date (*Balanite aegyptiaca*) kernel

## 📊 Summary of Experimental Data
* **Optimal Feedstocks:** Beef Tallow and Balanite kernel present the most viable commercial yields (92.8% and 39.65% oil content respectively).
* **Blending Capabilities:** While raw variants exhibited deviations from parameters, engineered **B20 blends** successfully normalized properties within critical ASTM D6751 and EN 14214 thresholds.
